# Pro+ and Enterprise Extended Refund Policy

> Last updated 2025-09-15. Supersedes refund-policy-2024.md for Pro+ and Enterprise customers.

## Extended window for Pro+ / Enterprise

Customers on **Pro+** or **Enterprise** plans get a **30-day refund window** instead of the standard 14-day window described in `refund-policy-2024.md`.

> **NOTE FROM SUPPORT LEAD (2026-01-12):** This is the source of recurring confusion. The 14-day doc was never deprecated; it still applies to Free, Starter, and Pro tiers. Pro+ launched in 2024-Q3 and inherited a 30-day window negotiated with Finance. If you read this and the 14-day doc gives a different answer for the same customer plan, escalate per escalation-criteria §7.

## Pro+ specific

- 30-day refund window from any new charge or upgrade
- Includes add-ons (different from the standard policy — Pro+ add-ons ARE refundable within 30 days)
- Annual Pro+ plans: prorated, no processing fee

## Enterprise specific

- 30-day window
- Custom contract terms supersede this doc — check the customer's signed agreement first
- All Enterprise refunds require both Finance and the AE to approve

## Plan tier reference

| Plan | Refund window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Free | n/a | nothing to refund |
| Starter ($29) | 14 days | per refund-policy-2024 |
| Pro ($79) | 14 days | per refund-policy-2024 |
| Pro+ ($199) | 30 days | this doc |
| Enterprise (custom) | 30 days, supersede by contract | this doc |
