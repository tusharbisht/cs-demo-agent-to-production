# Refund Policy

> Last updated 2024-03-01.

We offer a **14-day refund window** from the date of any new subscription or upgrade. Customers may request a refund through support; tier-1 escalates to Finance for approval (per escalation-criteria §1 if amount > $200).

## Eligibility

- New subscriptions (first month) — eligible within 14 days of charge
- Plan upgrades — eligible within 14 days of upgrade charge for the *delta amount*
- Annual plans — prorated to unused months, less a 10% processing fee

## Not eligible

- Renewals after the first year
- Add-on charges (Analytics, Storage, etc.) — those are usage-based and non-refundable
- Trial-to-paid conversions if the trial-end notification was acknowledged

## Process

1. Customer requests refund via support
2. Tier-1 verifies eligibility against this policy
3. If amount ≤ $200 and clearly within window → tier-1 may issue (with two-step confirmation)
4. If amount > $200, or any ambiguity → escalate to Finance, who responds within 1 business day

## Refund methods

Original payment method only. We do not issue store credit or transfer to a different account.
