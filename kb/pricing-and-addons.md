## Common invoice questions

**"Why is my invoice higher than the plan price?"**
Most often: an add-on was enabled mid-cycle. The first invoice after add-on enablement shows the prorated charge plus the next month's full charge.

**"I was charged twice"**
Check for: (a) annual auto-renewal coinciding with a monthly charge that didn't disable, (b) plan upgrade mid-cycle producing a delta charge. If neither applies, escalate to Finance.

## Trials

- 14-day trial of any paid plan
- Trial → paid conversion charges the day after trial end
- We send a reminder 3 days before trial end; "I didn't see the email" requires manual prorate

> Tier-1 agents: any "I want a refund because I forgot to cancel my trial" request follows the standard refund policy. Don't make exceptions on the floor. If amount > $200 → Finance per escalation §1.
