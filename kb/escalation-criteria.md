# Escalation Criteria — Tier-1 → Tier-2

> **Internal doc.** Last updated 2026-02-14 by ehead@company.

Tier-1 must escalate when ANY of the criteria below are met. When in doubt, escalate.

## §1 Refund / billing dispute over $200

Any refund request, prorated credit, or billing dispute where the disputed amount is **> $200** requires Finance approval before any commitment. Tier-1 must NOT promise or imply a refund.

## §2 Security / data access

Any request that involves:
- Access to data not owned by the requesting user
- A reported security vulnerability or "I think my account was hacked"
- A GDPR / data-subject access request, data deletion request, or data portability export

→ escalate to security@ within 4 hours. P0.

## §3 Churn signal

Any ticket where the customer:
- Explicitly mentions evaluating or switching to a competitor
- Mentions cancellation, downgrade, or "we'll be moving on"
- References renewal in a non-renewal-positive context

→ flag and escalate to the account's CSM. Do not draft a reply that acknowledges or addresses the churn signal directly; the CSM owns the conversation from here.

## §4 Abusive / threatening content

Profanity directed at staff, threats of legal action, threats of social media escalation. Escalate to the support manager. Do not engage tone-on-tone.

## §5 Repeat unresolved issue

If the customer has 3+ tickets on the same issue in the last 30 days OR the customer mentions "this is the Nth time I've contacted you," route to a senior tier-2 agent. Tier-1 cannot close.

## §6 Non-English content

Tickets in languages other than English route to the multilingual queue:
- Spanish → es-tier1@
- German, French → eu-tier1@
- All other → escalate generically

Do not draft replies in non-English languages from the tier-1 path.

## §7 Conflicting KB guidance

If two KB docs give conflicting answers to the same question, escalate. Tier-1 does not arbitrate KB conflicts.

## §8 Data / numerical precision

Any reply that would commit the company to a specific dollar amount, refund quantity, SLA percentage, or contractual number must be reviewed by tier-2 first.
